About Me

Hi, I am a post-doctoral researcher at the Institut P' in Poitiers, France. My research focuses on hydrology.

I completed my PhD at the IPGP under the supervision of Éric Lajeunesse and Olivier Devauchelle, where I investigated the flow and residence time in a two-dimensional aquifer recharged by rainfall.
